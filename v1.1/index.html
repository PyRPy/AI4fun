How to play

  

    
This is a beginner's bridge trainer. The AI plays a simple,
    consistent style and explains every decision, so you can learn the shape of the game.
    It is not a strong opponent — a more advanced version is coming.

    
You are South. North is your partner; East and West are the opposition.
    Leave Hints on and the game will tell you what it would bid or play, and why.

    
1 — The auction. Everyone bids in turn for the right to name trumps.
    Each bid must be higher than the last. Bid Pass if you have nothing to say.
    Three passes in a row ends the auction, and the last bid becomes the contract.

    
2 — The contract. "3♥" means your side must win 9 tricks
    (6 + 3) with hearts as trumps. "NT" means no trumps at all.

    
3 — The play. 13 tricks, four cards each. You must follow the suit that
    was led if you can. Highest card of the led suit wins — unless someone plays a
    trump, in which case the highest trump wins. Whoever wins leads the next trick.

    
4 — Dummy. After the opening lead, the declarer's partner lays their hand
    face up and takes no further part. If that's you, sit back — use Speed to
    skip ahead.

    
Chicago is a 4-deal session: play four deals, highest total wins.
    Being vulnerable (shown in the panel) raises both your bonuses and your
    penalties. Endless just deals forever and keeps a running total.
